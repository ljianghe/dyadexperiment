Instructions

You are Player {{ current_player_number }}.

This game will be played against a former participant playing this exact game.

You each start with 20 tokens.

In each trial, you will be asked to choose either “Red” or “Black,” and your payoff is shown in the payoff table.

Payoff table

Player 2: RedPlayer 2: Black
Player 1: Red1, -1-1, 1
Player 1: Black-1, 11, -1

Note: first entry = payoff to Player 1; second entry = payoff to Player 2.

One way to understand this graph: If both players choose the same color, Player 1 earns one point. If they choose different colors, Player 2 earns one point.

Depending on the outcomes listed in this figure, the experimenter will move the tokens accordingly on a trial-by-trial basis after all trials have been completed.

Your objective is to earn as many tokens as possible.

You will play 150 rounds or until one participant loses all their tokens. At the end of the game, you will earn $0.10 for every token you have.
