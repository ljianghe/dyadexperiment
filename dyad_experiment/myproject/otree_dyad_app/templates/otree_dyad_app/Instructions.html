Instructions

You will participate in a series of competitive games with the other member of your dyad. The final outcome of the game is dependent on the joint choices between both players in a dyad.

You each start with 20 tokens. When instructed, choose to play either a red or black card without showing or knowing the opponent’s move. You will then reveal to each other what your responses are. You will be informed which player number you are in accordance with the figure below.

Depending on the outcomes listed in this figure, either collect or give one token to the opposing player. Your objective is to earn as many tokens as possible.

Payoff matrix

Player 2: RedPlayer 2: Black
Player 1: Red1, -1-1, 1
Player 1: Black-1, 11, -1

Note: first entry = payoff to Player 1; second entry = payoff to Player 2.

You will play 150 rounds or until one participant loses all their tokens. At the end of the section, you will earn $0.10 for every token you have.

We will go through a few practice trials to ensure understanding of the game.
