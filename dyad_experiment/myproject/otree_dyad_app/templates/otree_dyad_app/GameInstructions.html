Instructions

You are Player {{ player_number }}.

You each start with 20 tokens. When instructed, choose to play either a red or black card without showing or knowing the opponent’s move. You will then reveal to each other what your responses are.

Depending on the outcomes listed in this figure, either collect or give one token to the opposing player. Your objective is to earn as many tokens as possible.

One way to understand this graph: If both players choose the same color, Player 1 earns one point. If they choose different colors, Player 2 earns one point.

You will play 150 rounds or until one participant loses all their tokens. At the end of the game, you will earn $0.10 for every token you have.
