Instructions

You will participate in a series of competitive games with the other member of your dyad.

The final outcome of the games is dependent on the joint choices between both players in a dyad.

For this game, you are Player {{ current_player_number }}.

You each start with 20 tokens.

In each trial you will be asked to make a decision of either “Red” or “Black” and your payoff is shown in the payoff table.

Payoff table

Player 2: RedPlayer 2: Black
Player 1: Red1, -1-1, 1
Player 1: Black-1, 11, -1

Note: first entry = payoff to Player 1; second entry = payoff to Player 2.

Depending on the outcomes listed in this figure, the experimenter will move the tokens accordingly on a trial-by-trial basis after all trials have been completed.

On each round, you may use the optional fair Red/Black randomizer coin to aid you in making your choice. You do not have to use it, and its result does not automatically select your response.

Your objective is to earn as many tokens as possible.

You will play 150 rounds or until one participant loses all their tokens. At the end of the game, $.5 will be awarded for every 1 token that each player has.
